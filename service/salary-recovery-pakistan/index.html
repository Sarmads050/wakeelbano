Salary Recovery in Pakistan

How to recover unpaid salary in Pakistan: the claim route under the Payment of Wages Act and the labour court, gratuity and provident fund, provident fund records, and the evidence that proves arrears.

Unpaid wages are recovered by a claim in the labour court or under the Payment of Wages Act where it applies, supported by salary slips, bank credits and attendance records. Gratuity, provident fund and other statutory dues are usually claimed in the same proceedings.

Key facts

Statutes
Payment of Wages Act 1936; provincial industrial relations laws; Employees' Old-Age Benefits Act
Forum
Labour court, or the authority under the Payment of Wages Act
Proof of arrears
Salary slips, bank statements, attendance records, employer's ledger
Other dues
Gratuity, provident fund, leave encashment, notice pay
Limitation
Short — check the statute immediately
Best evidence
A bank statement showing the monthly credit, then a gap

How do you prove arrears?

By showing what was payable and what was not paid: the agreed salary, the period worked, and the absence of credit in the bank.

ClaimEvidence
Unpaid salarySalary slips, bank statements, appointment letter, attendance
OvertimeAttendance or duty rosters, wage rate
GratuityLength of continuous service, last drawn wage
Provident fundFund statements, deductions shown in salary slips
Notice payContract, termination letter, date of departure
Preparation tip: build a one-page calculation sheet — month, amount due, amount paid, shortfall. Courts and employers both settle faster when the arithmetic is on one page.

What should a lawyer do in the first week?

Freeze the evidence, calculate the claim, and send a written demand to the employer.

  1. Collect every salary slip, bank statement and message before the client's access is cut off.
  2. Prepare the calculation sheet and get the client to sign it.
  3. Send a written demand with a deadline, by a mode that gives proof of delivery.
  4. Confirm the forum and the limitation period.
  5. File, and claim interest or compensation where the statute allows it.

What if the employer says the worker abandoned the job?

The burden shifts to the record: attendance, leave applications, messages and witnesses decide whether the worker left or was pushed out.

  • Attendance registers and gate records.
  • Leave applications and approvals.
  • Messages showing the employer's instructions up to the last day.
  • Witnesses who saw the worker on site after the alleged abandonment.

Frequently asked questions

Can salary be recovered after the company closes?

Recovery becomes harder but is not impossible — the entity's status, its assets and the liability of office-bearers matter. Act quickly.

Is gratuity payable to every employee?

Entitlement depends on the applicable statute, the length of service and the employer's coverage. Check the specific law for that establishment.

Do I need a lawyer for a small claim?

Not always, but a well-drafted demand and a correct calculation often produce a settlement without litigation — and that is usually the fastest money for the client.
