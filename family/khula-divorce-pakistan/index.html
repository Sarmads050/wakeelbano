Khula and Divorce in Pakistan

How khula and divorce work in Pakistan: the family court suit, the union council notice, the 90-day reconciliation period, the return of dower, and the mistakes that delay a decree.

Khula is a dissolution of marriage sought by the wife in the family court. Divorce by the husband is effected by pronouncing talaq and giving written notice to the union council, which then starts a 90-day reconciliation period before the divorce takes effect.

Written notice to the union council concerned
Reconciliation period
90 days under the 1961 Ordinance

The court considers its return; it is not an automatic condition
Usual documents
Nikah nama, CNIC copies, marriage photographs, notice receipts

What is the difference between khula and talaq?

Khula is initiated by the wife through the court; talaq is pronounced by the husband and becomes effective through the notice procedure.

KhulaTalaq
Who starts itThe wife, by a suit in the family courtThe husband, by pronouncing talaq
Court involvementRequired — the court passes the decreeNot required to pronounce, but notice to the union council is required
TimingEnds when the court dissolves the marriageEffective after the 90-day period, unless the wife is pregnant
DowerThe court considers whether it should be returnedDower remains payable unless already paid

How does a khula suit actually proceed?

Suit on the prescribed form, notice to the husband, a reconciliation attempt, the wife's statement, then a decree dissolving the marriage.

  1. File the suit in the family court with the nikah nama and the correct court fee.
  2. The court issues notices and attempts reconciliation under the Family Courts Act 1964.
  3. The wife's statement is recorded; in khula the court is not bound to the husband's consent.
  4. The court frames the terms — dower, maintenance, custody — and passes a decree.
  5. Send the decree to the union council so the record is updated and the iddat period runs correctly.
Drafting habit: plead the dower position and custody in the same suit. Splitting them into separate cases costs the client money and time.

What does the husband's talaq require?

A written notice to the chairman of the union council, a copy to the wife, and then the statutory 90-day reconciliation period.

  • The notice must be in writing and delivered to the union council; keep the receipt and a copy.
  • The union council constitutes a reconciliation council and informs the wife.
  • The divorce takes effect on expiry of 90 days, or on the birth of a child if the wife is pregnant.
  • The wife's maintenance during iddat and the dower position should be settled in writing at the same time.
  • Registration of the divorce with the union council is a separate step — do not confuse it with the notice.

Which mistakes delay a decree the most?

Wrong forum, wrong suit form, an incomplete nikah nama, an unserved address, and mixing criminal and family proceedings without a plan.

  • Filing as a civil suit instead of on the family court form.
  • Address problems — service failing because the address is a shop or a relative's house.
  • Dower left blank in the plaint, so it cannot be decided in the same decree.
  • Custody forgotten until after the decree, requiring a second case.
  • Anger in the pleadings — allegations that cannot be proved weaken the case the court must decide.

Frequently asked questions

Can khula be granted against the husband's will?

The court can dissolve the marriage on the wife's suit. The husband's consent is not a legal condition, although the court considers whether the dower should be returned. Follow the practice of your own court.

How long is iddat and does maintenance continue?

Iddat is normally three menstrual cycles, or until delivery if the wife is pregnant. Maintenance during iddat is a separate entitlement from maintenance pending suit; plead both.

Does a khula decree end the husband's duty to pay child maintenance?

No. A child's maintenance is the child's right and is decided separately from the marriage. Raise it in the decree rather than leaving it for a later case.
