Maintenance in Pakistan

Maintenance for a wife, children and parents in Pakistan: what the court considers, how quantum is calculated, interim maintenance, the evidence to produce, and how arrears are enforced.

A husband must maintain his wife and children, and a person of means must maintain parents who cannot support themselves. Pakistani courts fix the amount on the husband's income and the family's standard of living, and interim maintenance can be ordered while the suit continues.

Key facts

Statutes
Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961, sections 9 and 9-A; Family Courts Act 1964
Claimants
Wife, children, and in some cases parents
Key test
Means of the husband against the needs and standard of living of the family
Interim relief
Available while the suit is pending
Evidence
Salary slips, bank statements, tax returns, property documents, business records
Enforcement
Arrears are recoverable; the court can order payment and, in some cases, attachment

How does a court decide the amount?

It compares the husband's proven means with the family's reasonable needs and their prior standard of living; there is no fixed formula.

Courts look at income, visible assets, business profits and lifestyle, then compare them with rent, school fees, medical costs and food. A husband cannot reduce maintenance by simply claiming a low salary while his lifestyle shows otherwise.

FactorWhat the court looks at
IncomeSalary slips, bank statements, tax returns, business accounts
Assets and lifestyleProperty, vehicles, foreign travel, school of the children
NeedsRent, utilities, education, medical expenses
Other dependantsExisting legal obligations of the husband

Can a client get maintenance before the case ends?

Yes. Interim maintenance can be granted while the suit is pending, and it is often the most useful order in the file.

  1. Plead interim maintenance in the suit or by a separate application.
  2. Attach evidence of the husband's income — salary certificate, bank statement, tax return, or a business record.
  3. Attach evidence of the family's expenses — rent agreement, school fee vouchers, medical bills.
  4. Ask for the order to run from the date of application and to be paid monthly on a fixed date.
  5. If it is not paid, apply for enforcement immediately rather than waiting for the next date.

What documents make a maintenance case strong?

Anything that proves income and anything that proves the family's expenses — both sides of the equation.

  • Income — salary slips, employment letter, bank statements, tax returns, business registration, property documents.
  • Expenses — rent agreement, utility bills, school fee receipts, medical prescriptions and bills.
  • Status — nikah nama, CNIC and B-form for children, and the birth certificate.
  • Conduct — written demands for maintenance and any reply received.
Evidence tip: a screenshot of a salary slip sent on WhatsApp is weak; a bank statement showing the credit is strong. Teach the client the difference before the hearing.

Frequently asked questions

Does a working wife lose her right to maintenance?

Not automatically. Her own income is a factor the court weighs, not an automatic bar. Plead and prove the standard of living the family had.

Can parents claim maintenance from their children?

Yes. A person with means is obliged to maintain parents who cannot support themselves. The claim is usually brought in the family court.

What if the husband ignores the maintenance order?

Apply for enforcement of arrears and ask for the remedies the court provides, which can include attachment of property. Keep a running account of unpaid amounts.
