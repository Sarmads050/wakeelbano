What are the three decisions that matter most?

Pick one court, keep your living cost low enough to survive a small stipend, and build a referral network deliberately rather than hoping for one.

  1. Choose a court. City Courts, Malir or the Sindh High Court. Mastery of one court beats rushing between five.
  2. Control your rent. A chamber seat share plus a shared room near the court is the cheapest workable combination.
  3. Build your network on purpose. Chamber seniors, the bar room and your batchmates are the three channels that actually send work.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way for a new lawyer to live near the courts?

A chamber seat share, typically Rs 6,000 to 15,000 a month, combined with a shared room at Rs 8,000 to 15,000. It cuts the daily commute and the monthly cost at the same time.

Where are legal jobs in Karachi advertised?

Mostly they are not. Chambers hire through visits and referrals. Firms and companies do advertise, but the hidden market is much larger.
