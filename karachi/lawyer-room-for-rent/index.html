Wakeel ke liye Room / Seat Share — Karachi

Naye wakeel ki sab se bari tension: rehail. Rs 30,000 ki income par Karachi mein room milna mushkil hai — magar network wale log ye masla hal kar lete hain. Hum wahi network formal bana rahe hain.

Rs 30,000 stipend par: rehail 12–18k + khana 8–12k + transport 4–6k = pehle mahine hi tight. Is liye seat share + shared room ka combination sab se practical hai.

Kaun se ilaqay court ke liye behtar hain

  • Punjab Colony / Garden — City Courts aur Saddar ke qareeb, lawyers ka ghana network, khana sasta
  • Saddar — har jagah se transport, magar raat mein shor aur bheer
  • Jamshed Road / Patel Para — darmiyani budget, court aur SHC dono ka access
  • Malir — Malir court ke liye, sasta, magar City Courts door
  • Gulshan-e-Iqbal — behtar rehail, magar roz safar aur bara kharcha
  • Clifton / Defence — high court ke qareeb, magar junior ke budget se bahar

Room lete waqt 8 cheezein check karein

  1. Malik ki identity — CNIC dekhein aur note karein (owner hai ya sub-letter?)
  2. Rent agreement — likha hua, 2 copies, duration aur advance clear
  3. Bijli ka bill — separate meter hai ya shared? Rate kaun tay karta hai?
  4. Paani — tanki, timing aur quality (Karachi ka sab se bara issue)
  5. Security — gate, guard, lock, aur raat mein entry ka system
  6. Deposit ki wapsi — kab aur kis condition par? Likhein.
  7. Notice period — kitne mahine pehle batana hoga (dono taraf)
  8. Study/work environment — file rakhne ki jagah, light, internet
Khatoon lawyers ke liye: family building, warden, security guard aur separate entrance zaroor check karein. Sirf "safe hai" kehne par bharosa na karein — visit karein, doosri residents se poochein.

Broker ke bajaye kya karein

  • Chamber ke seniors aur colleagues se poochein — unke paas khali seat ka pata hota hai
  • Law college ke alumni group aur bar room notice board
  • Direct owner se deal karein — "commission" dene se pehle sochein
  • WakeelSaathi Rehail listings — verified, area aur budget filter ke saath

⚠️ Kabhi bhi room dekhne se pehle advance/booking payment na karein. Advance hamesha agreement ke baad.
