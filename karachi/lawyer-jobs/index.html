How do you find the jobs that are never advertised?

Visit chambers in person, ask your batchmates and seniors, watch the bar room notice board, and use verified listings rather than waiting for a job post.

  • A senior or classmate who already knows your work
  • Chamber visits with a printed CV, which work better than email
  • Notice boards in the bar room and near the courts
  • Your law college alumni group
  • Verified chamber listings on WakeelBano
Red flag: anyone charging a "placement fee" or promising a guaranteed job is almost always a fraud. Reputable chambers do not ask for money.

What should a one-page CV contain?

Name and contact details, a line on your practice interest, verified counts of your drafting and court experience, internships, skills and a sample draft.

  • Header: name, phone, email, city and one line on your area of interest
  • Replace objectives with evidence: "drafted 3 bail applications, 12 legal notices, observed 40+ appearances"
  • Internships: chamber name, court, dates and what you actually did
  • Skills: drafting types, research databases, languages
  • Education and status: LLB, Law-GAT, pupillage stage
  • One sample draft attached — it demonstrates ability faster than any claim

What should you expect to be paid?

Expect nothing to Rs 25,000 during pupillage, Rs 25,000 to 50,000 as a junior chamber associate, and more in firms and in-house roles.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my first legal job in Karachi?

Go in person. Take a one-page CV and a sample draft to chambers near the courts you want to practise in, and ask your seniors for introductions. Referrals fill most openings.

Do law firms hire fresh graduates?

Yes, but usually through internships first. A strong internship record and a writing sample matter more than grades alone.

Are remote legal jobs available in Pakistan?

Yes. Foreign firms and legal process outsourcers hire Pakistani lawyers for drafting, research and document review, usually paid in dollars. Strong English and a writing sample are essential.
