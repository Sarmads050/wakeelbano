Hidden job market — asli mauqay kahan hain

Karachi mein aam taur par 70–80% chamber positions advertise nahi hotin. Wo aise milti hain:

  • Senior ya classmate jo aap ko jaanta hai
  • Chamber visit — CV haath mein le kar (email se zyada kaam karta hai)
  • Court ke deewar/electric pole par lagay chhote notices, aur bar room ki notice board
  • Law college ka alumni group
  • WakeelSaathi jaisi verified listing jahan chamber apna zaroorat likhta hai
Yaad rakhein: Paisa maangne wale “job agent” fake hote hain. Koi serious chamber job ke liye pehle paisa nahi maangta.

CV jo kaam karti hai (1 page)

  • Header: naam, phone, email, city, aur ek line — kaunsa area
  • Objectives ki jagah "what I can do": "3 bail applications, 12 legal notices drafted, 40+ court appearances observed"
  • Internships — chamber ka naam, court, dates, kya kaam kiya
  • Skills: drafting types, research databases (PLD/SCMR, law sites), zaban (Urdu/English/Sindhi)
  • Education + enrollment status (Law-GAT, pupillage stage)
  • Ek sample draft attach — aap ki asli ability sab se zyada yahi dikhati hai

Salary expectations (realistic)

  • Pupil / trainee: Rs 0–25,000 (kai jagah zero)
  • Junior associate (chamber): Rs 25,000–50,000
  • Law firm associate: Rs 50,000–120,000+
  • In-house (0–2 saal): Rs 60,000–120,000
