Lawyer Chamber for Rent in Karachi

What a chamber seat costs in Karachi, what to check before sharing a chamber, how seat sharing works between juniors, and the areas closest to City Courts and the Sindh High Court.

A shared chamber seat in Karachi usually costs Rs 6,000 to 15,000 a month, depending on the court vicinity, the size of the chamber and whether a clerk is included. Always agree in writing what the seat includes before paying.

What does a chamber seat actually cost?

Between Rs 6,000 and Rs 15,000 a month for a shared seat, with the higher end near the High Court and in chambers with a library and clerk.

What should a junior check before sharing a chamber?

Who the tenant of record is, what the rent includes, how the key is held, whether the senior is present daily, and whether client files will be safe.

  • Tenancy — the chamber's own lease and the person legally entitled to sublet space.
  • Inclusions — electricity, internet, cleaning, clerk, stationery. Get a list, not a promise.
  • Security — a locker or cupboard you alone can lock, and a written inventory.
  • Use of the library — borrowing rules, and whether reports may leave the chamber.
  • Notice — how many days must either side give before leaving.
  • Client meetings — whether you may meet your own clients there, and at what hours.
Do not pay a deposit without a receipt that names the payer, the amount, the period covered and what the deposit secures.

How does seat sharing between juniors work in practice?

Two or three juniors take one chamber, split the rent and utilities, and agree a rota for who receives clients and how files are stored.

  1. Agree the split in writing: rent, utilities, clerk, stationery.
  2. Allocate desks and storage physically, so nothing is ambiguous.
  3. Set rules on client meetings — booked slots, no overlapping appointments.
  4. Keep separate files, in separately locked cupboards.
  5. Fix an exit clause: 30 days' notice, deposit returned after the final utility bill.

Is a chamber seat worth the money in the first year?

Yes, if it puts you within walking distance of your court and gives you a place clients can visit. It is a cost of doing business, not a luxury.

Compare it with the alternative:

  • Working from home means no walk-in clients and no library.
  • Paying for a separate room costs two to three times more for the same utility.
  • A shared seat near the court saves travel time, which is the scarcest resource of a junior.

On a Rs 30,000 stipend, a Rs 8,000 seat is 27% of income — but it is the difference between being a lawyer with an address and one without.

Frequently asked questions

Can a chamber seat be shared by a man and a woman advocate?

Shared professional space is a matter for the parties to agree, and many chambers allocate separate desks and storage. What matters is a written arrangement that both sides are comfortable with; harassment at the workplace is separately governed by law.

Do I need a separate chamber to take my own clients?

No, but you need a place where a client can sit and where your files are safe. Confirm with your senior or co-sharers that you may meet your own clients there.

What is a fair deposit for a chamber seat?

Commonly one month's rent. Anything higher should be justified in writing and covered by a receipt that states the terms of return.
