Where can Pakistani lawyers find case law for free?

The Supreme Court and every High Court publish judgments free on their official websites. Commercial databases add citation search on top of that.

What is the fastest reliable way to research a section?

Read the bare Act first, list each element as a separate issue, then look for five to eight leading judgments that deal with those issues.

  1. Frame the section. Read the exact text of the bare Act and write the elements out as separate issues.
  2. Hunt headnotes. Search the section number with the key words you identified. Prefer Supreme Court authority, then your own High Court.
  3. Extract the ratio. One line per judgment: the principle the court applied. You rarely need a full case summary.
  4. Distinguish. Write down how your facts differ from each authority. This is the step AI cannot do for you, and the step judges test.
Verification rule: Check every citation in three places — the citation number, the operative part of the judgment, and its current status after any appeal or review.

Why does WakeelBano not reproduce judgments?

Because a wrong or invented citation damages a lawyer in court. We explain the framework and the method, and link you to the official source where the judgment can be read.

Reported judgments sit inside law reporters that carry their own editorial rights, and AI tools sometimes generate citations that do not exist. Instead of reproducing text, we give you the statutory framework, an issue map, the research method and a verification checklist — then link to the court's own publication.

Can I cite an authority I found through an AI tool?

Not without checking it. Open the original judgment, confirm the citation and the operative paragraph, then check whether it has been reviewed or distinguished.
