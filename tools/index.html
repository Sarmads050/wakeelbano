Court tools

Three things a junior needs every court day: your own cause list, the next working date, and what filing actually costs. Everything runs inside your browser — nothing you type is sent to a server.

1. Cause-list tracker

Your matters, your dates. Saved on this device only, so client names stay with you.

2. Court-day calculator

Count working days between two dates, or find the date N working days from today — skipping weekends and the holidays below.

Holidays used in the count (2026)

Islamic dates depend on moon sighting and are announced by the government a day or two before. This list is a planning aid — confirm with your court office and the official notification before telling a client a date.

3. Filing cost worksheet

Adds up what a fresh civil suit usually costs to file. Court fee is an estimate — the stamp vendor or the court office gives you the exact figure.

Read this before you rely on the court fee. The Court Fees Act 1870, Schedule I, Article 1 sets an ad valorem fee on the value of the subject matter, and the percentage and the ceiling have both been amended over the years. Treat the number above as a planning estimate only: the stamp vendor and the court office decide the exact fee, and a wrong fee comes back as a scrutiny objection.
