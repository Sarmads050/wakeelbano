Karachi pilot

Punjab Colony se shuru kyun?

Karachi ke court areas ke aas paas lawyers ka sab se ghana network hai — City Courts, Saddar, Garden, Punjab Colony, Gulshan. Naye lawyer ko yahan teen cheezein chahiye: sasta rehail, qareeb chamber, aur aisa banda jo raasta bataye. Teeno ek hi mohalle mein mil sakti hain.

  • Verified rooms, PG aur seat-sharing listings — broker ke bina
  • Chamber-share: ek hi chamber, 2–3 juniors, rent split
  • Mohalle ke seniors ka mentor network — "jo dost hote hain" wala system, formal banaya hua
  • Mahine ka kharcha plan: rehail + khana + transport + bar fee = realistic budget

Guardrails

Chaar rules — in ke baghair AI aap ko nuqsan dega

Rule 1

Citation verify karein

AI ka diya hua case ya section khud parhein. Fake citation court mein aap ki izzat kha jaata hai.

Rule 2

Client data kabhi nahi

Naam, CNIC, address, case number — AI mein kabhi na daalein. Anonymise karein.

Rule 3

Draft, phir sudhaarein

AI first draft deta hai; final draft aap ka hota hai. Har clause par apna judgement lagayein.

Rule 4

Zimmedari aap ki

Court mein jo file jaata hai, us ka banane wala aap hain — tool nahi.
