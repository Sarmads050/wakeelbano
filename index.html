Why WakeelBano exists

Every new lawyer hits the same three walls

01

"Where do I find a senior?"

Six months of pupillage is mandatory, but no bar council arranges the placement. Finding a chamber still depends on personal contacts — and a newcomer to the city has none.

02

"I am not being paid"

Fresh lawyers typically earn Rs 25,000–50,000 a month, while the Sindh minimum wage is Rs 43,000. Many chambers pay nothing and call it a stipend.

"Where does my first client come from?"

Drafting is learned by copying precedents, marketing is never taught, and years pass without a brief. The problem is not talent — it is the absence of a system.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is WakeelBano?

WakeelBano is a career platform for Pakistani lawyers, starting in Karachi. It helps law students, fresh graduates and junior advocates find a chamber and mentor, paid drafting work, lawyer jobs across Pakistan, and free case law research guides.

Is WakeelBano free for junior lawyers?

Yes. Students, fresh graduates and junior advocates use the platform free. Chambers, law firms, universities and sponsors pay for listings, subscriptions and workshops.

Does WakeelBano give legal advice?

No. It is a technology platform that provides career resources, listings, research guidance and tools to lawyers. It does not give legal advice, represent clients, or take commission on client case referrals.

How do I find a chamber for pupillage?

Pupillage is mandatory, but placement is not arranged by bar councils, so personal contact matters. Filter verified chambers by practice area, court, language and terms — including whether a stipend is offered — then send a request directly.

What do Pakistani lawyers search for most?

Case law is the most searched topic, followed by the Law-GAT, bar council enrollment and drafting formats such as bail applications and legal notices. Our research library covers each of these.

Guardrails

Four rules, or AI will hurt you

Rule 1

Verify every citation

Open the judgment yourself. A citation that does not exist destroys your credibility in one hearing.

Rule 2

Never paste client data

No names, CNIC numbers, addresses or case numbers. Anonymise before you prompt.

Rule 3

Draft first, then edit

AI produces a first draft. The final draft is yours, clause by clause, with your own judgement.

Rule 4

The responsibility is yours

Whatever is filed in court is your work, not the tool's. Act accordingly.

Growth school

Your first client comes from the internet

Clients search for lawyers on Google, Facebook and YouTube long before they call anyone. The skill is doing it without breaking professional conduct rules.

Allowed

What you can do

  • Explain the law in plain language — video, post or blog
  • Comment on public judgments and changes in the law
  • State your education, experience and practice areas
  • Run free legal awareness sessions and clinics
  • Use client reviews with permission and no case details
Careful

What you should avoid

  • False claims such as "the best lawyer in Karachi"
  • Guaranteeing the outcome of a case
  • Sharing a client's identity or case details
  • Publicity inside the court or in front of a judge
  • Taking cases through touts or middlemen
