What is WakeelBano?

WakeelBano is a Pakistani career platform for lawyers: chamber and mentor matching, a legal job market covering all of Pakistan, paid work, legal research guides and an AI kit for drafting.

WakeelBano is a career platform for Pakistani lawyers, built for Karachi first. It helps new lawyers find a chamber and mentor, paid drafting work, legal jobs across Pakistan, and reliable research guidance — all free for junior lawyers.

Who is WakeelBano for?

Law students, fresh graduates, pupils and junior advocates in Pakistan, plus the chambers, law firms and universities that hire them.

  • Law students who need to know what to do before and after graduation
  • Fresh graduates facing the Law-GAT, pupillage and enrollment process
  • Junior advocates looking for chambers, paid work and better visibility
  • Seniors and firms who need reliable juniors and a verified public profile
  • Law colleges that must place students for mandatory internships

How does WakeelBano make money?

From chambers, firms, universities and sponsors — not from junior lawyers, who use the platform free.

What does WakeelBano not do?

It does not give legal advice, does not take commission on client case referrals, and does not publish or sell lawyer rankings.

Referral fees for client cases would amount to touting, which is prohibited under professional conduct rules. Any "best lawyer in Pakistan" ranking would be misleading. We stay on the correct side of both lines: information, tools and verified connections between lawyers.

How is content on this site produced?

From primary sources — bare Acts, bar council notifications and court publications — with the source named on every page and an explicit verification instruction.

Our guides explain statutory frameworks and research method. We do not reproduce reported judgments, and we do not invent citations. Every page carries a date, a scope note and links to the official source you can check yourself.

Where is WakeelBano based?

Karachi, Sindh, with an initial focus on City Courts, the Sindh High Court and the districts around them.

The pilot starts in Karachi because that is where the largest concentration of new lawyers practises, and where the gap between a law degree and paid work is widest. Expansion to Hyderabad, Sukkur, Lahore and Islamabad follows once the Karachi network is verified and working.

Frequently asked questions

Is WakeelBano a law firm?

No. It is a technology platform. It does not provide legal advice, does not represent clients and does not act as an agent in any case.

Is WakeelBano free?

Yes for students, fresh graduates and junior advocates. Chambers, firms, universities and sponsors pay for listings, subscriptions and workshops.

Does WakeelBano take a cut of a lawyer's fee?

No. It never takes a share of a lawyer's professional fee, and never takes commission on client referrals.

Which cities does it cover?

Karachi first, with Sindh districts next, then Lahore and Islamabad.
