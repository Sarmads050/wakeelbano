What the job involves

Banking and finance associate work: facility agreements, security documents and due diligence.

How to apply

Partner-track hiring runs through the firm's own portal — apply there, not through the aggregator.

  1. Open the original listing. The button on the right (and at the bottom of this page) takes you straight to Legalalphabet (aggregator — confirm on employer page), where the application is actually made. WakeelBano is not an employer and takes no fee from candidates.
  2. Keep the papers ready. One-page CV, LLB/LLM degree, Sindh Bar Council enrolment certificate, CNIC, and for litigation roles a short drafting sample.
  3. Write the first line for the reader. Name the exact role, your enrolment year, and one case or transaction you handled. Two sentences are enough.
  4. Follow up once. If there is no reply in a week, send one short email. Do not send a third.

Before you apply

  • Confirm the last date on the source page — that listing is the only reliable date, and some employers close early once they have enough applicants.
  • Check the experience requirement against your own record: this post asks for Associate. If you are one year short, apply anyway and say why you are ready — many chambers are flexible about the number and firm about the writing.
  • Never pay money for a legal job or pupillage. No genuine chamber charges a joining fee, and a request for one is a scam.
  • Keep a record. Note where and when you applied, so a follow-up is easy and the next interview does not catch you out.
