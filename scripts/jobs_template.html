If you are looking for work

  1. Browse jobs and tasks. Filter by city and area of law.
  2. Build your profile once — skills, cases, chamber, rate.
  3. Apply at the original source for aggregated jobs; apply here for tasks and posted roles.
  4. Complete the work, get rated, and let the rating build your record.

If you are hiring

  1. Post the role or task with a clear fee and a clear scope.
  2. Read applications and profiles — cases handled, not adjectives.
  3. Agree the work in writing, then mark it complete.
  4. Rate the lawyer. Ratings are what make the market trustworthy.

Straight answer on "is anything missing": no aggregator sees everything — many chambers in Karachi and Lahore never advertise at all, and placements still move through contacts. What we do guarantee is that every listing published here is real, dated and linked to its source, and that the list is reviewed monthly. If you know of an opening that is not here, post it in three minutes using the "Post a job" tab and it appears immediately.

Why this exists: a new lawyer's first problem is not knowledge, it is access — to a chamber, to a first brief, to anyone who will pay for a draft.
