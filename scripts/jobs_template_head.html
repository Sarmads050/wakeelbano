What recruiters get

  • Applications from lawyers with a visible case record, not just a CV
  • Filter by city, area of law, skills and rating
  • Rate the lawyer after the work is completed — one review per job
  • Freelance tasks with escrow, so you pay only for work delivered
  • No commission on client case referrals — that stays prohibited
For chambers: pupillage postings are free for the first year. A six-month seat is the single hardest thing for a new lawyer to find, and it should not cost a chamber money to offer one.

If you are looking for work

  1. Browse jobs and tasks. Filter by city and area of law.
  2. Build your profile once — skills, cases, chamber, rate.
  3. Apply at the original source for aggregated jobs; apply here for tasks and posted roles.
  4. Complete the work, get rated, and let the rating build your record.

If you are hiring

  1. Post the role or task with a clear fee and a clear scope.
  2. Read applications and profiles — cases handled, not adjectives.
  3. Agree the work in writing, then mark it complete.
  4. Rate the lawyer. Ratings are what make the market trustworthy.
Why this exists: a new lawyer's first problem is not knowledge, it is access — to a chamber, to a first brief, to anyone who will pay for a draft. This page puts every opening in one list and gives a junior lawyer a public record of work completed.
