Affidavit Format (Pakistan)

How to prepare an affidavit in Pakistan: the deponent, statement of truth, jurat, attestation before a notary and the mistakes that make an affidavit worthless.

An affidavit is a written statement of facts that the deponent swears or affirms to be true before an authorised officer. It must identify the deponent, state facts in numbered paragraphs, show the place and date of swearing, and carry the jurat and the attestation.

Key facts

Statutes
Oaths Act 1873; Code of Civil Procedure 1908, Order XIX
Who can attest
Notary public, oath commissioner, or a court officer empowered to administer oaths
Contents
Facts only, in the deponent's own knowledge unless stated on belief
Numbering
Paragraph-wise, complete sentences, no argument or opinion
Annexures
Documents exhibited and referred to in the body
Consequence
A false affidavit exposes the deponent to proceedings for perjury

What is the structure of a valid affidavit?

Title, description of the deponent, numbered facts, annexures, jurat, and the attestation block.

  1. Title — court and case, or, for a general affidavit, the purpose in one line.
  2. Deponent details — full name, age, CNIC number and occupation, and place of residence.
  3. Opening — that the deponent is competent to swear the affidavit and understands the facts.
  4. Numbered paragraphs — one fact per paragraph, chronologically ordered.
  5. Source of knowledge — say where the fact comes from, and mark believed facts as stated on belief.
  6. Exhibits — each document named and marked as an annexure.
  7. Jurat — sworn or affirmed at a named place on a stated date, before a named officer.
Common failure: the deponent signs but the officer's attestation, seal or licence number is missing. That single omission can push an affidavit out of consideration.

What must the jurat say?

It must record that the contents were read over and explained to the deponent, that the deponent understood them, and that the oath was taken before the named officer on a stated date.

A workable jurat in Pakistani practice reads, in substance:

Verifiedthat the contents were read over and explained to the deponent, who understood them, and that the statement is true to the best of the deponent's knowledge, information and belief.
Sworn / affirmedat [place] on [date] before [name and designation of the attesting officer].
Signatureof the deponent, with the officer's signature, stamp and licence number.

Where the deponent cannot read, the officer must record that the affidavit was read and explained in a language the deponent understands.

Which mistakes make an affidavit risky?

Opinion instead of fact, facts the deponent cannot know, legal argument, no exhibit labels, and conflicting statements in an earlier affidavit.

  • Opinion and law — the affidavit is for facts; arguments belong in the plaint or application.
  • Hearsay without disclosure — always state the source.
  • Over-claiming — anything false or reckless invites proceedings for perjury and destroys credibility.
  • Unlabelled annexures — refer to documents by annexure letter in the body.
  • Stale templates — depends on the court; a district court affidavit and a High Court affidavit differ in heading and sometimes in format requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Can an affidavit be sworn in English if the deponent only speaks Urdu?

It can, but the officer must record that it was read over and explained in a language the deponent understood. Otherwise the affidavit is open to challenge.

Does an affidavit need stamp paper?

In Pakistani practice court affidavits are usually prepared on plain paper and attested by a notary or oath commissioner, while some purpose-specific affidavits are executed on stamp paper. Confirm the local requirement with the office of the court or the sub-registrar.

Is an affidavit evidence on its own?

Not automatically. It is used where the law or the court's order permits, and the deponent can still be required to appear for cross-examination. Read Order XIX CPC and the court's specific order.
