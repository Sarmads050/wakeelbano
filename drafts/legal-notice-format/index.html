Legal Notice Format (Pakistan)

How to draft a legal notice in Pakistan: the nine required parts, how to prove delivery, what a 15-day period really means, and the mistakes that weaken a notice.

A legal notice is a formal written demand that sets out the facts, the default, the exact relief wanted and a deadline. Written properly, it ends many disputes before a suit is filed.
WakeelBano · KarachiUpdated: October 2026Reading time: about 6 minutes

Key facts

Typical deadline
15 days is common practice, but statutory periods vary
Proof of delivery
Registered post with acknowledgement due, or tracked courier
Common uses
Recovery of money, cheque dishonour, rent, contract breach, defamation
No government fee
The cost is professional fees and postage

When should a legal notice be sent?

Before litigation, whenever a demand can still resolve the dispute, and whenever a statute requires prior notice as a condition for filing.

  • Recovery of money — unpaid loans, invoices or services
  • Cheque dishonour — before a 489-F PPC complaint
  • Rent and eviction — under the applicable rented premises law
  • Breach of contract — demanding performance or accepting termination
  • Property and possession — warning against unlawful dispossession
  • Defamation — demanding a retraction

What are the nine parts of a legal notice?

Letterhead and date, correct addressee, subject line, chronology, the default, the exact demand, the deadline, the consequence and a reservation of rights.

  1. Letterhead, date and reference number
  2. Full name and complete postal address of the addressee
  3. A specific subject line, such as "Legal notice for recovery of Rs ___"
  4. Chronology with dates: when the agreement was made, what was delivered, what remains due
  5. The default or breach, tied to a clause or section
  6. A precise demand: the exact amount or action
  7. A deadline, commonly 15 days
  8. The consequence of non-compliance, including costs
  9. Reservation of rights and the advocate's signature

How do you prove the notice was delivered?

Send it by registered post with acknowledgement due or a tracked courier, and keep the receipt, the tracking record and a copy of the notice in your file.

In court, the first question is not what the notice said but whether it was served. Email or WhatsApp notices only work where the contract or the law allows them, and even then you must keep the send confirmation and a screenshot.

Note: the date on the notice and the date of dispatch are different things. Courts look at both.

Is a 15-day period always required?

No. Some statutes prescribe their own period, such as suits against the government, so check the specific section rather than assuming 15 days.

Fifteen days is a common practice for commercial demands. Where a statute sets a period, that period governs, and the notice must comply with its form as well as its timing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a legal notice cost?

There is no government fee. The cost is the advocate's professional fee plus registered post or courier charges. Court fee applies only when a suit or complaint is filed.

Can a legal notice be sent by WhatsApp?

Only where the contract or the law permits electronic service, and only with proof of delivery saved. Registered post or courier remains the safer default.

Is a notice required before a cheque dishonour complaint?

Practice and the facts of your case determine this. Check the section, the current approach of the courts, and keep proof that your demand reached the other side.
