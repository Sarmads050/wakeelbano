Bail Application Format — Pakistan

Bail application wo pehli drafting hai jo har junior lawyer se maangi jati hai. Format ration se nahi aata — structure aur grounds se aata hai.

WakeelSaathi · KarachiLast updated: October 2026Reading time: ~5 min

Pehle section samjhein — bailable, non-bailable, aur konsa bail?

SectionKis surat mein
496 CrPCBailable offence mein bail (aam taur par as of right)
497 CrPCNon-bailable offence mein bail — court ki discretion, aur kuch cases mein special rules
498 CrPCSessions Court / High Court ko bail ka power
491 CrPCIllegal detention ke khilaf (habeas corpus)
426 CrPCConviction ke baad appeal mein suspension of sentence/bail

Pehle FIR ke sections dekhein aur tay karein ke offence bailable hai ya non-bailable — ghalat section par application dene se waqt zaya hota hai.

Application ka standard structure

  1. Heading: Court ka naam, case number/FIR number, police station, section(s), aur parties.
  2. Title: "Application for grant of bail under section ___ CrPC on behalf of the accused".
  3. Brief facts: 4–6 lines — FIR version, accused ka role, arrest ki date, custody ki muddat.
  4. Grounds: numbered — har ground ek separate legal point.
  5. Legal basis: sirf wahi sections/authorities jo aap ne khud verify kiye hain.
  6. Prayer: clear — "accused ko bail par release kiya jaye" + conditions.
  7. Verification + affidavit + vakalatnama attached.

Grounds jo court sunta hai (facts ke saath, sirf dawa nahi)

  • Investigation mukammal — accused se mazeed recovery/pur-tashaddud ki zaroorat nahi
  • Bail ke baad farar ka koi asar nahi — roots in society, permanent address, family
  • Evidence kamzor ya sirf suspicion — record se uthaye gaye sample points
  • Delay in FIR / delay in arrest — dates ke saath
  • Co-accused ko bail mil chuki hai — parity, judgment ka reference
  • Medical / age / health — documented grounds
  • Remand aur custody ki muddat — trial mein deri
Practice tip: Grounds ko facts se bandhein: "accused 3 din se custody mein hai, challan pesh ho chuka hai, record par koi recovery nahi" — aisi lines court zyada sunti hain.

Common mistakes jo application kamzor karti hain

  • Bail aur "acquittal" ki baat mix karna — bail ka maqsad trial ke doran release hai, bari nahi.
  • Grounds mein poori FIR dobara likhna (justice ke liye facts ka summary kaafi hai).
  • Bina verified authority cite karna.
  • Prayer vague chhorna — court ko exactly batayein kya chahiye.
  • Court fee/stamp aur annexures ka adhoora set.

Court fee, stamp aur annexures

Court fee aur stamp paper ki zaroorat court aur case ki qism ke hisaab se badalti hai. Mutaliqa court ke filing branch se current requirement confirm karein — aur application ki copies (court + opponent) tayyar rakhein.
