Vakalatnama Format — Pakistan

Vakalatnama chhoti document hai — magar adhoori hone par case ki pehli date par hi masla aa jata hai.

WakeelSaathi · KarachiLast updated: October 2026Reading time: ~5 min

Vakalatnama mein kya hona chahiye

  1. Court ka naam aur case number/title
  2. Client ki details — naam, parentage, CNIC, address
  3. Advocate ki details — naam, enrollment number, chamber address
  4. Authorization clause — "I authorize the above-named advocate to appear, plead and act on my behalf" — scope likha ho (appear, plead, act, compromise?)
  5. Acceptance — advocate ka acceptance (kai courts mein zaroori)
  6. Signature + date — client (aur advocate)
  7. Stamp/court fee — court ke rules ke mutabiq
  8. Witnesses / attestation — jahan required

Scope of authority — sab se ahem clause

"Appear, plead and act" ka matlab har cheez nahi hota. Agar client chahta hai ke advocate compromise ya settlement kar sake, to ye alag se likha jana chahiye. Isi tarah:

  • Appeal file karne ka haq
  • Evidence led karne ka haq
  • Case withdraw karne ka haq
  • Kisi doosre advocate ko substitute karne ka haq
Practice tip: Scope ko italic/underline na karein — bas clear English mein likhein. Court aur client dono ko ek hi cheez samajh aani chahiye.

Court ke hisaab se farq

CourtNote
District / subordinate courtsAam format; stamp/court fee court ke rules ke mutabiq
Sindh High CourtHigh Court advocate ka enrollment number; filing branch ka current requirement check karein
Supreme CourtAdvocate-on-Record / Advocate Supreme Court ki requirement; apna makhsoos format
Tribunals (service, tax, etc.)Har tribunal ka apna practice; pehle filing branch se poochein

Common mistakes

  • Case number ghalat ya khali
  • Client ka naam CNIC se match nahi karta
  • Advocate ka enrollment number nahi likha
  • Acceptance clause missing — filing branch wapas kar deti hai
  • Pata adhoora — notice/order client tak nahi pahunchta
  • Stamp/fee ki qism ghalat

Vakalatnama cancel ya change karna

Agar client advocate badalna chahta hai, to courts ka apna process hai (no objection / discharge application). Ye process court ke rules aur case ki stage par depend karta hai — bina cause list/order ki copy ke koi step na lein.
