Plaint Format (Order VII CPC)

What a civil plaint must contain in Pakistan under Order VII CPC: parties, cause of action, jurisdiction, valuation, court fee, limitation, prayer and verification.

A plaint is the document that starts a civil suit. Under Order VII CPC it must state the parties, the facts, the cause of action, jurisdiction, valuation, limitation and the relief sought.

What must a plaint contain?

The court, the parties, the subject matter, dated facts, the cause of action, jurisdiction, valuation and court fee, limitation, the relief, verification and the document list.

  • The court's name
  • Plaintiff and defendant details, with addresses
  • The suit's title and subject matter
  • The cause of action: when, where and how it arose, with dates
  • Jurisdiction: territorial and pecuniary, with reasons
  • Valuation and the court fee paid
  • The relief claimed, quantified where money is involved
  • Limitation
  • Verification and the list of documents

How should the plaint be structured?

Follow the court's expected order: heading, parties, subject line, numbered facts, cause of action, jurisdiction and valuation, limitation, prayer, verification and annexures.

  1. Heading: "In the Court of ___" with the suit number
  2. Parties: plaintiff versus defendants, with addresses
  3. Subject: one line, such as "Suit for recovery of Rs ___"
  4. Facts: numbered paragraphs in date order
  5. Cause of action: a separate heading
  6. Jurisdiction and valuation: a separate paragraph
  7. Limitation: a separate paragraph
  8. Prayer: numbered reliefs, plus costs
  9. Verification, affidavit and documents

Where do valuation and court fee go wrong?

Under-valuing the suit or paying insufficient court fee leads to objections and delay, so confirm the current schedule at the filing branch.

  • Court fee paid too low — an objection follows, and the date is lost
  • Wrong valuation — raises a question over pecuniary jurisdiction
  • Unquantified relief — creates a problem at the decree stage

Court fee rules are set by statute and revised over time. Confirm the current position with the court, not with an old template.

What are the common mistakes?

Mixing facts with the cause of action, omitting dates, leaving the relief vague, forgetting the document list and skipping the limitation paragraph.

Each omission becomes the first objection raised against the suit. A plaint that answers all nine requirements in order survives scrutiny and reaches the merits faster.

Frequently asked questions

What is the format of a plaint in Pakistan?

Under Order VII CPC: court, parties, subject matter, numbered facts, cause of action, jurisdiction and valuation, limitation, prayer, verification and a list of documents.

How much court fee is payable?

It depends on the nature and valuation of the suit. Confirm the current schedule with the relevant court's filing branch.

Can a plaint be amended?

Generally yes, with the court's permission, but the earlier it is done the better. Amendments become harder once trial has progressed.
