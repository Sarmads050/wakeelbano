Power of Attorney Format (Pakistan)

Special and general power of attorney in Pakistan: what the Mukhtar Nama must state, attestation and registration, revocation, and the powers that are misused.

A power of attorney authorises one person to act for another. A special power of attorney is limited to particular acts, such as appearing in one case or executing one sale; a general power of attorney covers wider powers. It must identify the parties, list the powers, state the term, and be properly attested and, where required, registered.

Special (specific acts) and general (broad authority)
Execution
Signed by the donor, attested by witnesses, and notarised
For court work
The vakalatnama is used for representation, not a power of attorney
For property

Revocation
In writing, communicated to the holder, and often registered if the original was registered

What must a power of attorney contain?

Identity of donor and holder, an exact list of powers, any limits, the term, and a clear statement of when the authority ends.

  1. Title — Special or General Power of Attorney, and its purpose.
  2. Parties — donor and attorney with full names, parentage, CNIC and address.
  3. Recitals — why the authority is being given, in one or two paragraphs.
  4. Operative clauses — numbered powers. Be exact: to sign, to sell, to appear, to receive.
  5. Limits — what the attorney may not do without fresh written authority.
  6. Term and revocation — duration and how it can be revoked.
  7. Execution block — donor's signature, witnesses, notary attestation, and registration endorsement where applicable.

Special or general — which one should you draft?

Choose the narrowest authority that does the job. A general power of attorney is the most abused document in property matters.

SpecialGeneral
ScopeOne transaction, one case, one actWide, continuing authority
RiskLowHigh — misuse, unauthorised sales, borrowed signatures
Use it forSelling one plot, appearing in one matter, collecting one paymentManaging affairs during a long absence, when there is no alternative
Lawyer's dutyIdentify the donor and confirm the instructions directlyInsist on registration, a short term, and accounting obligations
Standing practice: never execute a general power of attorney for immovable property on the strength of a phone instruction. Verify the donor in person or by a documented video call with CNIC.

How is it attested, registered and revoked?

Signed before witnesses and a notary; registered where the transaction requires it; revoked in writing and, ideally, by a registered deed of revocation.

  • Attestation — witnesses must be present and must sign; the notary records the donor's identity.
  • Registration — a power relating to immovable property is normally presented for registration before the sub-registrar.
  • Revocation — a written deed, delivered to the attorney and notified to the parties who dealt on the strength of it; register the revocation if the original was registered.
  • Death of the donor — the authority ends automatically. Ensure the record reflects it.

Frequently asked questions

Can a power of attorney be used to appear in court?

A lawyer appears on a vakalatnama. A power of attorney may authorise someone to instruct a lawyer or act in related matters, but the vakalatnama is the document courts require for representation. Do not mix the two.

Is a power of attorney executed abroad valid in Pakistan?

It can be, if it is executed and attested in the manner the law requires, and usually notarised and then authenticated through the Pakistani mission concerned. Confirm the current requirement with the sub-registrar and the consular section.

What if an attorney sells property beyond the powers granted?

The transaction can be challenged on the ground that the act was outside the authority granted. Both the attorney and the purchaser may face consequences. Act quickly and preserve the original deed.
