What WakeelBano will never do

  • Sell a "best lawyer" ranking
  • Send clients to whoever pays most
  • Charge a junior lawyer for anything
  • Show the source of every job and salary
  • Rate a lawyer only after completed work
  • Publish the method behind every number
  • Remove a listing the day it closes
