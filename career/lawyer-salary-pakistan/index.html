Sindh minimum wage se comparison — asli tasveer

Sindh mein unskilled worker ka minimum wage Rs 43,000 (2026-27) hai. Yani ek 5 saal padha hua law graduate aksar us se kam par kaam kar raha hota hai — aur kai jagah "salary" ke bajaye "stipend" keh kar minimum wage law ko bypass kiya jata hai.

Apne haq janу: Chamber terms likhwaayein. “Aap seekh rahe hain” ka matlab ye nahi ke kuch bhi na mile — kam az kam transport/kharcha aur clear learning scope tay karein.

Salary kam kyun hoti hai (structural wajah)

  • Lump-sum per-case fee: Pakistan mein hourly billing aam nahi. Senior ke liye junior ka waqt "revenue" nahi, "cost" hota hai.
  • Training period: Fresh graduate ko productive hone mein aam taur par 1–2 saal lagte hain.
  • Retention: Juniors 1–2 saal mein chale jate hain, is liye firms invest karne se katraate hain.
  • Supply: Har saal hazaron graduates, magar paid seats kam.

Income barhane ke 5 practical raaste

  1. Ek practice area mein specialise — "sab kuch" karne wale ki koi rate nahi hoti.
  2. Court-specific mastery — ek court, us ki practice, us ke judges ki expectations.
  3. Remote legal work — foreign firms ke liye drafting/research (Upwork, direct clients). Rates USD mein.
  4. Content + visibility — jo wakeel online samajh aata hai, us ke paas referral aata hai (rules ke andar rehte hue).
  5. Kaam Board — khali waqt mein seniors ka overflow drafting/research kaam.

Negotiation script (jo kaam karti hai)

Interview/chamber meeting mein: "Sir, main 6 mahine aap ke saath serious commitment kar raha hoon. Main drafting, research aur court appearance par kaam karna chahta hoon. Iske liye minimum monthly ___ aur hafte mein ek din study/CPD ka rakh sakte hain?"

Specific demand + specific commitment = serious candidate. Sirf "kitna milega?" poochne wala candidate replace ho jata hai.
