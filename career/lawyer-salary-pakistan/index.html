Lawyer Salary in Pakistan (2026)

Realistic lawyer salary ranges in Pakistan for 2026: pupillage, junior associates, law firms and independent practice, compared with the Sindh minimum wage.

Most fresh lawyers in Pakistan earn between Rs 25,000 and Rs 50,000 a month, and many pupils are paid nothing at all, while the Sindh minimum wage for unskilled work is Rs 43,000.

What are the realistic salary ranges?

Litigation chambers pay the least and law firms the most, with corporate and in-house roles paying more than chambers at every level of experience.

Why is the pay so low compared with the minimum wage?

Because Pakistani litigation uses a lump-sum fee per case rather than hourly billing, so a junior's time is a cost to the senior rather than billable revenue.

  • Lump-sum fees: there is no hourly billing culture in most chambers, so juniors are not revenue producers
  • Training period: a fresh graduate typically needs one to two years to become useful
  • Retention: many juniors leave within two years, which discourages investment
  • Supply: thousands of graduates enter the market each year for a limited number of paid seats
Know your position: put the chamber terms in writing. "You are here to learn" does not mean you cannot agree a minimum contribution towards expenses and a clear scope of training.

How can a junior lawyer increase income?

Specialise in one area, master one court, take remote drafting work from foreign firms, build a visible professional profile and use idle hours for paid research and drafting.

  1. Specialise. A lawyer who does everything has no rate.
  2. Master one court. Learn its practice, its filing staff and its judges' expectations.
  3. Add remote work. Foreign firms pay in dollars for drafting, research and document review.
  4. Be visible. Educational content within professional conduct rules brings referrals.
  5. Fill idle time. The Kaam Board on WakeelBano lists paid drafting and research tasks.

What is a good way to negotiate chamber terms?

Offer a specific commitment in return for a specific arrangement: hours, learning scope and a minimum monthly amount, agreed in writing.

A workable opening: "I can commit six months, I want to work on drafting, research and court appearances, and I would like a minimum of Rs ___ a month plus one study day a week." Specific candidates get taken seriously; candidates who only ask about pay are replaced.

Frequently asked questions

What is a fresh lawyer's salary in Pakistan?

Usually Rs 25,000 to Rs 50,000 a month, depending on the city and the firm. Many chambers pay no stipend during pupillage.

Can a chamber legally pay below the minimum wage?

This depends on how the relationship is classified and on the sector-specific rules. Many chambers treat juniors as trainees rather than employees. For your own position, take advice from a labour or employment lawyer.

How much do independent lawyers earn?

There is no fixed figure. The first year or two is often very low, and income rises with reputation and case flow. Many lawyers supplement it with teaching, drafting or freelance work.
