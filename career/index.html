Career: From Sanad to the High Court

The full Pakistani legal career path: Law-GAT, bar council enrollment, pupillage, realistic salary ranges and the High Court licence — stage by stage.

Becoming a practising lawyer in Pakistan takes about six years: five years of LLB, the Law-GAT, a six-month pupillage and enrollment with a provincial bar council.

How long does each stage take?

Five years for the LLB, one test cycle for the Law-GAT, six prescribed months of pupillage, then two years of practice before a High Court licence.

Frequently asked questions

What is the first step after 12th grade?

Get admission to a recognised LLB programme. Before you commit, spend one summer interning in a chamber so you know what the work actually feels like.

Is the Law-GAT compulsory?

The prescribed test must be passed before enrollment as an advocate under Pakistan Bar Council rules. Confirm the current requirement with your provincial bar council.
