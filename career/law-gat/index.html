Law-GAT 2026 — Pakistan ka Law Graduate Assessment Test

LLB ke baad sanad ka pehla darwaza: Law-GAT. Har saal hazaron graduates isi test ke intezaar mein hote hain — aur sab se zyada search bhi yahi hoti hai.

WakeelSaathi · KarachiLast updated: October 2026Reading time: ~5 min

Law-GAT kya hai?

Law-GAT (Law Graduate Assessment Test) ek assessment test hai jo Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan Bar Council ke saath mil kar leta hai. Iska maqsad ye check karna hai ke law graduate ke paas practice ke liye bunyadi legal knowledge hai ya nahi.

Pakistan Bar Council ke rules ke mutabiq advocate enroll hone ke liye law degree ke saath prescribed test pass karna zaroori hai. Test generally saal mein do baar hota hai — magar exact dates, fees aur registration window har cycle mein badalte hain.

Sab se pehle ye karein: HEC ki official website par current Law-GAT notification dekhein. Kisi bhi WhatsApp forward ya blog ki date par bharosa na karein — including is page par.

Eligibility — kaun apply kar sakta hai

  • Recognized university se LLB (ya equivalent, jo Pakistan Bar Council ne recognize kiya ho)
  • Final year ke students bhi aksar apply karte hain — current notification check karein
  • CNIC / identity documents aur degree/transcript ki details
  • Prescribed test fee — bank challan ya online (cycle ke hisaab se)

Foreign degree holders ke liye HEC/PBC ki equivalence requirement alag hoti hai — pehle wo process complete karein.

Syllabus — kis cheez par focus karein

Law-GAT aam taur par multiple choice questions par mabni hota hai aur bunyadi legal subjects cover karta hai. Aam taur par in areas par focus hota hai (current syllabus HEC se confirm karein):

AreaKya revise karein
Constitutional lawFundamental rights, writ jurisdiction, key constitutional provisions
Criminal lawPPC ke bunyadi sections, qatl/hurt/property offences, CrPC procedure
Civil lawCPC (plaint, jurisdiction, appeals), Contract Act, Specific Relief
EvidenceQanun-e-Shahadat 1984 — relevancy, burden of proof, documentary evidence
Family & personal lawMFLO 1961, dissolution of marriage, custody, maintenance
Legal EnglishComprehension, legal terms, drafting basics

60-din ka study plan

  1. Din 1–10: Bare acts parhein — PPC, CrPC, CPC ke key sections. Notes flashcards mein banayein.
  2. Din 11–25: Subject-wise MCQs. Har ghalti ka reason likhein, sirf answer nahi.
  3. Din 26–40: Past papers (timed). Weak areas ki list banayein.
  4. Din 41–52: Weak areas + Qanun-e-Shahadat aur family law par focus.
  5. Din 53–60: Sirf revision + full-length mocks. Naya material nahi.

Result aur answer key kahan check karein

Result aur answer key sirf HEC ki official website/portal se check karein. Result ke din traffic bohat zyada hota hai — page slow ho to official portal par thori der baad try karein. Roll number / CNIC required hota hai.

⚠️ Result declare hone se pehle kisi "leaked result" link par click na karein — ye phishing ka aam tareeqa hai.

Test pass karne ke baad kya?

Law-GAT pass karne ke baad aap provincial bar council (Sindh mein Sindh Bar Council) mein advocate enrollment ke liye apply karte hain, jismein senior advocate ke chamber mein pupillage shamil hai. Us ka poora step-by-step plan hamare Bar Council enrollment guide mein hai.
