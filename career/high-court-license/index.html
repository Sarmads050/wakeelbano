High Court License (Advocate High Court)

Lower court se High Court — ye promotion nahi, naya license hai. 2 saal ka practice record aaj se maintain karna shuru karein, warna baad mein jodna mushkil hota hai.

WakeelSaathi · KarachiLast updated: October 2026Reading time: ~5 min

Eligibility

  • Advocate ke taur par enrollment (provincial bar council roll par)
  • Kam az kam 2 saal subordinate courts mein practice (Act ke section 27 ke mutabiq)
  • Prescribed application form + fees
  • High Court ki fitness/character requirements aur interview (agar required ho)

Exact requirements aur current fees Sindh High Court / Sindh Bar Council se confirm karein.

Process

  1. Practice record tayyar karein — 2 saal ke cases ki list: title, court, case number, aap ka role.
  2. Application (prescribed form) bar council ke through High Court ko.
  3. Certificates — good standing, dues clearance, aur character/fitness.
  4. Review / interview — court ke process ke mutabiq.
  5. Certificate issue — High Court advocate ke taur par enrollment.

Supreme Court tak ka raasta

Advocate Supreme Court banne ke liye aam taur par High Court advocate ke taur par 7 saal ki practice aur prescribed process (fitness certificate, interviews, fees) chahiye. Detail hamesha Pakistan Bar Council se confirm karein — requirements aur fees waqt ke saath badalte hain.
